Project Overview

This project seeks to redefine prostheses from solely functional devices often associated with negative connotations, to symbols of positive empowerment. It aims to enlighten the public on the significance of prostheses in their users' lives, promoting a perspective of pride and aesthetic appreciation.

“A pros·the·sis /präsˈTHēsəs/, or a prosthetic implant, is an artificial device that replaces a missing body part, which may be lost through trauma, disease, or a condition present at birth.” This project showcases an example with an above-knee leg prosthesis.

Project Ideation

Ameba is a BESO (Bi-directional Progressive Structural Optimization) software developed as a Rhino - Grasshopper plugin for structural optimization which is experimented in this project for form development. A cost-effective, structurally efficient, and highly customizable solution is achieved by using such method. In a given design space in Ameba, the loads, supports and other boundaries are defined and processed. Ameba uses Kpa (N/mm2) as its unit.

According to the ISO Standard 10328:2006 for lower limb prostheses, the two main loading conditions are toe-off and heel strike. The pylon is expected to withstand a compressive proof load of 1,395 N and a maximum load of 3,220 N.
